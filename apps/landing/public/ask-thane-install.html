Setup

1. Approve Slack access

Pick the workspace and complete the Slack OAuth flow.

2. Invite Thane

Add Thane to the channels where follow-up should happen with /invite @Thane.

3. Assign work naturally

Ask questions, assign tasks, and let Thane send follow-ups when work goes quiet.
